About This Tool

This tool estimates a patient's probability of remaining in buprenorphine-naloxone treatment over time using a random survival forest model developed from de-identified electronic health record data.

The chart compares the patient prediction with high-risk, low-risk, and median reference curves. The research underlying this tool is described in the publications listed under Citations.
